Evade

Evade-is a popular game in Roblox, the game was created 2022 August 4th and every year they celebrate the birthday of the game by putting some unique things and skins to the game. The official developers of the game are: beef(Beefchopets) and Oster(Osterdog) this information is found in the game settings. In the game you can buy coke and things to use in the game. Also they have Daily Shop where you can buy accesories, skins and many other things!

Halloween
